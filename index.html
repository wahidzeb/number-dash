About Number Dash

Number Dash is a running game where the puzzle is maths. Your Number Buddy runs down a track, and you steer through gates that add, take away, multiply or divide. Finish with a bigger number than the Number Monster to win.

There are six worlds and sixty levels, with maths settings for different ages, so younger children and older players can both enjoy it.

3 quick tips

  1. Blue gates (+ and ×) make your number bigger. Red gates (− and ÷) make it smaller.
  2. When both gates are blue, adding usually wins while your number is small, and multiplying wins once it is big.
  3. With two red gates, choose the one that takes away less.

How to play

Drag left and right (or use the arrow keys) to steer your Number Buddy.

Gates change your number: + and × make it grow, − and ÷ make it shrink. Think fast: sometimes +10 beats ×2, sometimes ×2 wins!

Quiz doors ask a quick sum. Run through the right answer to add it to your number.

Dodge the grumpy minus clouds, grab coins, and be bigger than the Number Monster at the finish.

The bigger your number at the end, the more stars you earn. Spend coins on new Buddies!

Pick Easy (adding and taking away), Medium (times tables) or Hard (big sums and dividing) in Settings.
